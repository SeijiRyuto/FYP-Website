🎣 Phishing Attacks

Fake QR codes can direct you to counterfeit login pages that steal your credentials.

🦠 Malware Downloads

Scanning can automatically download harmful software, giving attackers access to your device.

📍 Location Tracking

Some malicious codes trigger silent location requests, revealing where you live or work.

Stay safe. When in doubt, don't scan.
