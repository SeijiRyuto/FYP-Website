How QR Codes Track Your Location

Some malicious QR codes are crafted to trigger a geolocation request as soon as the page loads. If you grant permission (or if it’s silently granted via a webview), the attacker obtains your precise coordinates.

  • The technique: The hidden website runs JavaScript to call navigator.geolocation.getCurrentPosition().
  • The misuse: Attackers can build a profile of your daily movements, home address, and workplace.
  • Further risks: This data can be sold to advertisers, stalkers, or used for physical theft.

Real‑world example: In 2021, a QR code campaign on fake parking tickets used location data to identify car owners and sent them phishing texts.
